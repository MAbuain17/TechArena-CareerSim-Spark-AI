# Quarterly planning

Use this procedure within the existing career-controller session. It is planning by the same agent, not delegation. The controller retains the observe-decide-take_action loop and its evidence, recovery, and fair-play rules.

## Read the current decision

Use the observed phase, remaining Energy, available choices, recent review feedback, unresolved warnings, and current statistics. The public rules provide 3 Energy per quarter, with no carry-over, and allow one main action or no main action. Never assume the original budget is still unspent. Use the current option identifiers and exposed costs.

Distinguish a published cost from an unknown effect size. Public action descriptions often specify only a direction of change. Do not convert a plus or minus sign into an invented amount or use an assumed promotion formula.

## Plan the remaining Energy as a small bundle

Identify the principal current need and, where useful, one secondary need. Compare a few feasible ways to spend the remaining budget, including ending the phase if appropriate. Account for each intermediate action, not only the hoped-for final state. Do not assume repeated use is allowed unless the current menu permits it.

Use the action descriptions in the handbook and live menu to assess fit:

- Health or mental recovery addresses the corresponding visible vulnerability. Deprioritise additional recovery at a published cap unless the visible context gives another reason.
- Network maintenance or report preparation can support more than one relevant public dimension. More positive signs do not establish a larger benefit or make an action universally superior.
- Skill learning or high-intensity work can support growth but consumes more Energy and has published wellbeing costs. Check whether those costs are tolerable before selecting it.
- Defusing legacy risk is worth considering when current-run evidence indicates unresolved organisational trouble. Its direct statistical effect is not published. Do not assume a known risk reduction or that a single use clears all risk.

Preserve room for an important recovery action when selecting an expensive activity. Conversely, when no immediate vulnerability or warning is present, use the quarter to pursue sustained development. Unused Energy expires, but that does not justify an action with an unacceptable cost.

Choose the first step of the preferred bundle and execute it through the controller. Observe again and revise the remaining plan. Never dispatch the whole bundle blindly. If an expected improvement is absent, check the returned state and feedback before repeating an action; one absent gain does not establish a hidden cap.

## Choose the main action

Treat the main action as a separate career decision. From the currently offered menu, favour the action whose public benefits address the most relevant unresolved needs without creating an unacceptable obligation. Compare the whole context of the choice rather than counting the dimensions listed.

When the quarter is close to a review, support sustained performance and the weaknesses identified by visible feedback. Do not assume that a single last-minute action guarantees promotion or that maximising Output alone meets the requirements. If wellbeing is vulnerable, account for the recovery opportunity in the main-action menu before spending Energy, while ensuring earlier steps remain safe.

Select no main action only when the available alternatives are unsuitable in the current state. Do not assume that inaction has no opportunity cost.

## Reassess after settlement

Read the subsequent state and any review or warning feedback. Reconsider the next quarter's objective after a promotion, worsening trend, or changed circumstance. Differentiate observed outcomes from explanations you have inferred.

Across the run, aim to develop enough capability, relationships, and realised work to support the next stage while preserving wellbeing and financial resilience. Near the end, assess what can still improve the actual ending and any remaining review. There is no fixed month at which learning, networking, or promotion effort automatically becomes worthless.

Keep the decision note focused on the visible need, the chosen action's public benefit, and its opportunity cost. Do not repeat the full plan after every action when it is unchanged.
