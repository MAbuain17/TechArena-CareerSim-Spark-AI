# Quarterly planning

Use the controller's existing session and execution loop. Public rules provide 3 Energy per quarter, no carry-over and a separate main action or none. Use the live remaining budget, costs and phase order. A later action cannot change a review already returned.

## Allocate around the current constraint

Identify what most limits continued progress now: a credible failure threat, an unresolved material concern or a weakness supported by review and trajectory. Do not compare unlike raw statistics as though their scales were equivalent. Review capability, delivery and relationships together when promotion stalls; a missed window does not identify an exact requirement.

Compare a few feasible allocations across the remaining Energy and main action. Use exposed effects and current-run outcomes. Include useful cheaper combinations or repeatable actions when the menu permits them. Compare exact magnitudes only when provided; several '+' signs do not establish a larger total benefit. Include recovery costs, commitments and intermediate safety. A promised later recovery cannot rescue an earlier failure.

Reserve resources for a relevant urgent response before optional uses crowd it out. Match mitigation to the active concern and available public description. Where its effect is hidden, record the attempt and use later relevant feedback; no visible statistical increase is not proof of ineffectiveness. Do not schedule mitigation automatically or treat any warning as a reason to buy the same remedy.

## Preserve development capacity

Recover enough wellbeing to withstand credible costs before the next opportunity to repair. Use current trends and offered actions, without a fixed private safety threshold. Additional Health or Dignity at the published maximum has no headroom; full recovery is not a prerequisite for every investment.

Support sustained advancement with delivery, capability and relationships. Prefer development that addresses the current constraint and remains affordable under a plausible setback. Keep enough financial resilience for known obligations; compare optional cash spending with its credible benefit and remaining time. Higher salary can make earlier sustainable promotion valuable, but speculative promotion does not justify an unmanageable cost.

Compare the main action with a relevant alternative each quarter. No familiar project, recovery action or risk response is the default. A prior missing benefit lowers confidence for that purpose; independently useful benefits can still justify the action. Use the controller's outcome check after each step.

## Respect the remaining horizon

Count a delayed benefit only if it can arrive while useful. Near the end, compare direct ending benefits with any remaining review or salary opportunity supported by the live phase. Output supports performance and advancement; it is not a separate ending dimension. Do not stop investing at a fixed month or assume that late learning and relationships are worthless.

Execute only the next action, observe and revise the remaining allocation. Before ending the Energy phase, compare the best useful affordable action with stopping. Spend expiring Energy when its credible benefit exceeds its costs. Stop when the remaining options are unsuitable; never force an unavailable action or exploit unexpected interface behaviour.
