# Build advancement and earnings

Career advancement improves both ending level and future salary. Seek sustained progress early, without assuming a project or a single Output burst guarantees promotion. Use the current handbook's salary and bonus rules. Consider time remaining when comparing salary gains and delayed investments.

After a review, separate grade, criticism, bonus and promotion outcome. Identify the most strongly evidenced constraint across delivery, capability, relationships and risk. Set a concrete next objective: restore reliable delivery, improve capability, rebuild a relevant relationship or resolve explicit exposure. Do not invent an exact promotion threshold or assume a missed promotion identifies one deficient statistic.

Choose actions that improve the constraint while preserving other requirements. Multi-benefit actions are attractive only when their effects are supported and costs manageable. Visible delivery and recognition both matter; neither constant self-promotion nor invisible work is a universal solution. A high raw Skill value does not establish readiness for the next level.

Before a review, prefer timely results when the other needs are adequately covered. Do not postpone development until the review month: performance considers sustained results and the review may already be settled. After promotion, reassess new feedback and resource trends; do not automatically spend the following quarter only on recovery.

When no urgent threat exists, actively invest rather than accumulating excessive safety at the expense of career growth. When a threat exists, seek a response that preserves as much productive progress as feasible. Review the objective when feedback or outcomes change, not merely because the next story is more vivid.
