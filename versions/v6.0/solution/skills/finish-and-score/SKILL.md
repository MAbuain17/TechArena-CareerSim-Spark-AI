# Convert remaining opportunities into ending value

Use the public ending dimensions: level, wealth, health and dignity, skill, risk control and relationships. Output is useful through performance and promotion rather than as its own ending dimension. Use current published scoring details if available; do not import final-score thresholds from another run.

Assess each investment by whether its payoff can arrive before the ending. Do not stop developing at month 37, assume month 42 is the last possible promotion or ignore a remaining window shown by the interface. Follow actual phases through month 48.

Compare attainable marginal improvements across dimensions using available evidence. Raw numbers have different scales: the smallest visible statistic is not automatically the best investment. Repair a serious weakness when it threatens survival or ending value, and avoid buying increases beyond published maxima. Retain useful late skill, relationship and wellbeing gains when they can affect the ending directly.

Avoid new obligations whose costs arrive now and whose benefits fall beyond the horizon. Preserve safety through the final action, not only to the start of the last month. If a promotion can still occur, compare its level and remaining salary benefit with direct ending improvements; do not chase an unsupported qualification claim.

At the returned ending, report the exact score and component results. Do not turn a successful ending into proof that each choice was optimal or claim an official score from a local trajectory.
